## description

```text
HypeDomino is real-time online dominoes, built for fast matches, bright effects and fair play.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• 1v1: two players, seven tiles each. No tile to play? Draw from the boneyard.
• 2v2: four players, partners sit opposite each other and every tile is dealt.
• Ranked: 1v1 and 2v2 each have their own rank and leaderboard, season after season.
• Casual: the same rules with no rating at stake.
• Custom rooms: invite friends with a link or a room code, set up the teams and play.

SCORE IN FIVES
Place a tile so the open ends add up to a multiple of five and your team scores that total. The first double to get tiles on both sides becomes the spinner and opens up to four ends. Play all your tiles first and your team scores the pips left in your opponents' hands. Matches are played to 365 points. Special cases, such as the opening tile and the 300 rule, are explained in the game.

FAIR BY DESIGN
• Tiles are shuffled on our servers, and nobody ever sees a tile they do not hold.
• The server checks every move, every point and every reward.
• Nothing for sale changes your rank, your tiles or the rules. No pay-to-win.
• If a player drops out, a bot plays their turns until they come back, so the match goes on.

MAKE IT YOURS
Collect characters, avatars, frames, tables, emoji and lobby actions. Show them off at the table and in the friend lobby, where your 3D characters meet between matches. Every shop offer shows exactly what you get.

PLAY TOGETHER
React with emoji and quick messages during the match. Add friends, see who is online and invite them to your room.

SEASONS AND PROGRESS
Earn coins by playing. Complete daily and weekly missions, unlock the rewards of the season pass and collect medals.

GOOD TO KNOW
• An internet connection is required.
• No ads.
• Available in English, Azerbaijani, Turkish and Russian.
• Accounts are for players aged 13 and older.
• Optional in-app purchases: coin packs, the premium season pass and offers. Coins have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn.
```
