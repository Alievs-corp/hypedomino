## 1. Where you pay

You pay only through Google Play on Android and the App Store on iOS. They are the merchant of record: they take the payment, apply your local price and taxes, and handle refunds under their own terms. We never receive your card or bank data. There is no other way to pay for anything in the game.

## 2. What you can buy

| Product | What it gives |
|---|---|
| Crystal packs | The number of crystals shown in the offer |
| Premium Hype Pass | The premium track of the pass season running at the time of purchase |
| Premium Hype Pass with levels | The premium track plus the number of pass levels shown in the offer |
| Starter offer | Crystals and the items shown in the offer, once per account, for a limited time after the account is created |

- Every offer shows exactly what it gives before you buy. Nothing bought with money has random contents.
- Your first purchase also gives a one-time first-purchase bonus shown in the game. It is given once per account.
- Crystals buy cosmetics and pass levels. Coins cannot be bought.
- Prices are set per country and shown by the store in your currency before you pay. Everyone in the same country sees the same price; we do not personalise prices.
- Where a store cannot charge in your country, real-money offers are not shown.

## 3. Who may buy

Purchases need a Google or Apple account that can pay. The family and parental controls of Google Play and the App Store apply to every purchase, including purchases that wait for a parent's approval. [[COUNSEL: CQ-4, purchases by players aged 13 to 17 in each launch market]]

## 4. How a purchase is delivered

1. You choose an offer in the game. Our server checks that the offer is available to your account.
2. The store takes the payment.
3. Our server checks the purchase with the store and gives you its contents once. Only then is the purchase confirmed to the store. A purchase we never gave you is refunded by the store.
4. A purchase that waits for payment or for a parent's approval is given when the store tells us it is paid.
5. A purchase the store confirms as paid is always given, even if the offer ended or purchases were paused in the meantime.

Your purchases belong to your account on our servers. Signing in on another device restores them; there is no separate restore step.

## 5. Hype Pass

- A premium Hype Pass belongs to the pass season running when the store records the purchase. You can buy it once per season; a second purchase in the same season is refused before you pay.
- Premium rewards of the levels you have already reached unlock at once.
- When the season ends, the premium track ends with it. Rewards you have earned stay yours.

## 6. Refunds

- Refunds of money are decided and paid by Google Play or the App Store under their refund policies. Ask them through your store account. Our support cannot refund money.
- Deleting your account does not refund any purchase.
- When a store refunds a purchase, we take back what it gave: its crystals, its items, any first-purchase or starter bonus tied to it, and for a Hype Pass the premium track and every premium reward you claimed from it.
- Items you bought with crystals stay yours. If the crystals of a refunded purchase were already spent, your crystal balance goes below zero and you cannot spend crystals until new purchases bring it back to zero or above. Coins are never taken back.
- If the store reverses a refund, we give the contents back.
- After several refunds within a set period, further purchases are blocked and the account is reviewed for payment fraud.

## 7. Problems with a purchase

If a purchase did not arrive or arrived wrong, contact us in the game under Settings, Support, or at [[LP-2: support email]] if you cannot open the game. We check the purchase with the store and correct your account through our records.

## 8. Changes and the end of the service

We may change offers, prices and contents for future purchases; a purchase already made keeps what it gave. [[COUNSEL: CQ-3, what players receive for unused crystals and the current pass season if the game shuts down]]
