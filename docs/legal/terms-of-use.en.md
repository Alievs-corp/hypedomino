- We may change, add or remove features, modes, rules, items and events, and we may stop the game for maintenance. Changes take effect for matches that start after them; a match in progress finishes on the version it started with.

## 5. Coins, crystals and items

- Coins are earned by playing and are never sold. Crystals come only from real-money purchases and what those purchases unlock. One does not convert into the other.
- Coins, crystals and items are a limited, personal, non-transferable licence to use content in the game. They have no cash value, cannot be withdrawn, exchanged for money or traded, gifted or moved to another account, and do not expire.
- Nothing you can buy gives an advantage in matches: coins, crystals and money buy cosmetics, chests (with coins only), pass levels, the premium pass track and a nickname change, nothing else. Nothing for sale changes your rank, rating, matchmaking, turn time, penalties or the rules.
- Items with random contents exist only in chests, which are bought with earned coins only, never with money or crystals. The game shows the chest's items, the chance of each rarity and the opens left until a guaranteed rare item before you open one.
- No match, room or event has an entry fee in coins with coins or items as the prize.
- We may retire an item from sale; you keep it. If we must switch an item off (for example for a content problem), you keep it in your inventory but cannot equip it, and the game equips a default item instead.
- If a ledger error gives you coins, crystals or items you did not earn or buy, we may correct it.

You can report a player's nickname or room name from their profile or the match end screen; misuse of reports can stop your reports from being considered.

Using the appeal does not limit any other right you have under the law.
